// JD parsed: Founding Engineer, B2B SaaS startup · must-haves: end-to-end ownership, zero-to-one building
AI    Tell me about a product or feature you built entirely from scratch. What was the problem, and how did you decide the approach?
Candidate I built a real-time notifications system at my last startup. We were getting complaints about missed alerts so I designed a WebSocket-based push system.
AI    Interesting. Why WebSockets specifically — what alternatives did you consider, and what made you rule them out?
Candidate We looked at polling and SSE. Polling felt too heavy on our infra. SSE was one-directional. WebSockets gave us bidirectional and lower latency.
AI    You mentioned "heavy on infra" — what did that mean in your context? What were the actual constraints you were working under?

When a candidate says something interesting — or evasive — HireIQ digs deeper. "Why that approach? What did you try first? What would you do differently?"

JD-GROUNDED
